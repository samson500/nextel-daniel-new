FAQ

Common questions answered

Nextel is a platform where you invest in call minutes, receive allocated phone numbers, and earn per minute of call activity through those numbers. Register, activate your account with a voucher, and start earning daily returns.

Register for free, set your ATM and transaction PINs, then activate your account using a voucher code. Once active, choose a package and your allocated phone numbers will be assigned immediately.

Earnings are calculated and updated every 3–4 hours based on call activity passing through your allocated phone numbers.

Each user gets a personal web page that displays their call activity, allocated numbers, and earnings. It is designed to feel like your own revenue generating platform, it allows you to track performance in real time, monitor earnings growth, and easily claim your profits while exploring opportunities to scale.

Yes, you can request a withdrawal at any time once you have sufficient earnings. A small processing fee applies to each withdrawal request.

Earn Now, Pay Later lets you access a package immediately and repay the package cost from your future earnings over an agreed period, so you can start earning right away without upfront payment.

We offer two packages: Basic ($10 — 300 call minutes) and Advance ($20 — 500 call minutes). Higher packages give you more allocated contacts and significantly greater earning potential.
